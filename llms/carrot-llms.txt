# Carrot Fertility

> Carrot is the leading global fertility and family-building benefits platform, helping employers, health plans, and members access inclusive, cost-effective, and personalized reproductive care.

Carrot provides fertility and family-forming support across 170+ countries. Our platform empowers people through every stage of their reproductive health journey — from preconception and pregnancy to menopause and parental return to work.

## Programs & Solutions

- [Carrot Rx Flex Program](https://www.get-carrot.com/blog/carrot-rx-flex-program): A pharmacy solution that lowers fertility medication costs by over 50%.
- [Carrot Sprints](https://www.get-carrot.com/fertility-benefit-solution/sprints): The first metabolic-fertility program designed to optimize pre-pregnancy health.
- [Fertility Preservation](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/fertility-preservation): Support for egg, sperm, or embryo freezing, guiding members through every step of the preservation process.
- [IVF Support](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/ivf): Comprehensive IVF support that prioritizes safer, cost-effective pregnancies through single embryo transfer and evidence-based programs.
- [Gestational Surrogacy](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/gestational-surrogacy): Expert support for surrogacy journeys, including guidance through financial, legal, and emotional steps of the process.
- [Adoption Support](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/adoption): Inclusive adoption assistance for all families, providing access to vetted agencies, legal experts, and emotional support resources.
- [Men’s Health](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/mens-health): Comprehensive reproductive health support for men, addressing fertility, hormonal balance, and emotional well-being.
- [Pregnancy & Postpartum](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/pregnancy): Global pregnancy and postpartum care offerings for members and employers.
- [Parenting & Return to Work](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/parenting-return-to-work): Programs designed to help new parents transition back to work with confidence and support.
- [Hormonal Health](https://www.get-carrot.com/journey/hormonal-health): Clinical and educational support for menopause and hormonal changes.
